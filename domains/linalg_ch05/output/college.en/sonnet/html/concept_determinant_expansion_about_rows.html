Determinant Expansion About Rows

Definition. Let $A = (a_{ij})$ be an $n \times n$ matrix. The $(i,j)$-minor $M_{ij}$ is the determinant of the $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ submatrix obtained by deleting row $i$ and column $j$ from $A$. The corresponding cofactor is

$$C_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} M_{ij}.$$

The cofactor expansion along row $i$ expresses the determinant as

$$\det(A) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}\, C_{ij} = \sum_{j=1}^{n} (-1)^{i+j}\, a_{ij}\, M_{ij}.$$

This formula holds for every choice of row $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, providing $n$ equivalent representations of the same scalar.

Worked Example. Expand $\det(A)$ along row 1 for

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -1 & 4 \\ 5 & 2 & 6 \end{pmatrix}.$$

The three minors are

$$M_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 4 \\ 2 & 6 \end{vmatrix} = (-6 - 8) = -14, \quad M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 \end{vmatrix} = (0 - 20) = -20, \quad M_{13} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = (0 + 5) = 5.$$

Applying signs $(-1)^{1+j}$ gives cofactors $C_{11} = -14$, $C_{12} = 20$, $C_{13} = 5$. Therefore

$$\det(A) = 3(-14) + 1(20) + 2(5) = -42 + 20 + 10 = -12.$$

Key Theorem (Row Independence of Cofactor Expansion). For any $n\times n$ matrix $A$ and any row index $i$,

$$\det(A) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}\, C_{ij}.$$

Moreover, for $i \neq k$, the alien cofactor sum vanishes:

$$\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{kj}\, C_{ij} = 0.$$

Proof sketch. Replacing row $k$ of $A$ with a copy of row $i$ creates a matrix with two identical rows; its determinant is zero by the alternating property. Expanding that determinant along row $i$ yields exactly the alien sum, establishing the result. $\square$

This theorem underlies the practical utility of cofactor expansion: by choosing the row with the most zeros, one minimizes the number of nonzero terms and reduces computational effort substantially.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import Matrix, symbols, det, cofactor_matrix

A = Matrix([[3, 1, 2],
            [0, -1, 4],
            [5, 2, 6]])

print("det(A) =", det(A))          # -12

C = A.cofactor_matrix()
# Verify row-1 expansion
expansion = sum(A[0, j] * C[0, j] for j in range(3))
print("Row-1 expansion =", expansion)   # -12

# Verify alien cofactor sum vanishes (row 2 entries, row 1 cofactors)
alien = sum(A[1, j] * C[0, j] for j in range(3))
print("Alien sum =", alien)             # 0
