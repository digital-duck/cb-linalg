Range Of Linear Transformation

Definition. Let $T: V \to W$ be a linear transformation between vector spaces $V$ and $W$ over a field $\mathbb{F}$. The range (also called the image) of $T$ is the set of all vectors in $W$ that are the image of at least one vector in $V$:

$$\operatorname{range}(T) = \{\, T(\mathbf{v}) \mid \mathbf{v} \in V \,\} \subseteq W.$$

One verifies that $\operatorname{range}(T)$ is a subspace of $W$: it contains $\mathbf{0}_W$ (since $T(\mathbf{0}_V) = \mathbf{0}_W$), and is closed under addition and scalar multiplication by linearity of $T$. The dimension of the range is called the rank of $T$, written $\operatorname{rank}(T)$.

Worked Example. Define $T: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ by the matrix

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix}.$$

The range of $T$ equals the column space of $A$, that is, all linear combinations of the columns of $A$. Row-reducing $A$:

$$A \xrightarrow{\text{RREF}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

Both rows contain a pivot, so the two columns $\mathbf{a}_1 = (1,4)^\top$ and $\mathbf{a}_2 = (2,5)^\top$ are linearly independent and span $\mathbb{R}^2$. Therefore $\operatorname{range}(T) = \mathbb{R}^2$ and $\operatorname{rank}(T) = 2$.

Key Theorem (Rank–Nullity). Let $T: V \to W$ be a linear transformation with $\dim V = n < \infty$. Then

$$\operatorname{rank}(T) + \operatorname{nullity}(T) = n,$$

where $\operatorname{nullity}(T) = \dim\ker(T)$. Proof sketch: Choose a basis $\{\mathbf{e}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{e}_k\}$ for $\ker(T)$ and extend it to a basis of $V$. The images of the extension vectors are linearly independent in $W$ and span $\operatorname{range}(T)$, giving $\operatorname{rank}(T) = n - k$. $\square$

In the example above, $n = 3$ and $\operatorname{rank}(T) = 2$, so $\operatorname{nullity}(T) = 1$, confirming the single free variable $x_3$ in the null space.

Lab Cell (SymPy).

from sympy import Matrix

A = Matrix([[1, 2, 3],
            [4, 5, 6]])

print("Column space (range) basis:")
for v in A.columnspace():
    print(v.T)

print("Rank:", A.rank())
print("Nullity:", A.shape[1] - A.rank())

Running this cell returns two basis vectors spanning $\mathbb{R}^2$ and confirms $\operatorname{rank}(T) = 2$, $\operatorname{nullity}(T) = 1$.
