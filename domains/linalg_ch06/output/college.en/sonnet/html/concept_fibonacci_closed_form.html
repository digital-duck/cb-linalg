Fibonacci Closed Form

Definition. The Fibonacci sequence $\{F_n\}_{n \geq 0}$ is defined by the linear recurrence

$$F_0 = 0, \quad F_1 = 1, \quad F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2} \quad (n \geq 2).$$

The closed form (Binet's formula) expresses $F_n$ directly as a function of $n$, without reference to prior terms:

$$F_n = \frac{\varphi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}},$$

where $\varphi = \dfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618$ is the golden ratio and $\psi = \dfrac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx -0.618$ is its conjugate. Both $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are roots of the characteristic equation $x^2 = x + 1$.

Proof sketch. Assume a solution of the form $F_n = r^n$. Substituting into the recurrence gives $r^2 = r + 1$, with roots $r = \varphi$ and $r = \psi$. By linearity, the general solution is $F_n = A\varphi^n + B\psi^n$. Applying initial conditions $F_0 = 0$ and $F_1 = 1$ yields $A = 1/\sqrt{5}$ and $B = -1/\sqrt{5}$, producing Binet's formula.

Worked example. Compute $F_7$.

Direct recursion gives $0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13$, so $F_7 = 13$. Using Binet's formula:

$$F_7 = \frac{\varphi^7 - \psi^7}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{(1.6180\ldots)^7 - (-0.6180\ldots)^7}{\sqrt{5}} \approx \frac{29.034 - (-0.034)}{2.236} = \frac{29.068}{2.236} \approx 13.00.$$

Because $|\psi| < 1$, the term $\psi^n/\sqrt{5}$ decays rapidly; for $n \geq 1$, $F_n$ is the nearest integer to $\varphi^n/\sqrt{5}$.

Key theorem (exponential growth rate). $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{F_{n+1}}{F_n} = \varphi.$ This follows immediately from Binet's formula, since $(\psi/\varphi)^n \to 0$.

Lab cell (SymPy).

from sympy import sqrt, Rational, simplify, Symbol, fibonacci

n = Symbol('n', integer=True, nonneg=True)
phi = (1 + sqrt(5)) / 2
psi = (1 - sqrt(5)) / 2

binet = (phi**n - psi**n) / sqrt(5)

# Verify against the built-in for n = 0..10
for k in range(11):
    val = simplify(binet.subs(n, k))
    assert val == fibonacci(k), f"Mismatch at n={k}"

print("Binet's formula verified for n = 0 … 10")
